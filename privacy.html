Bosnify Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short

  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking; no data is sold.
  • Your account is anonymous; an e-mail address is only kept if you link Google.
  • Data is stored in the European Union (Frankfurt) and sent encrypted.
  • You can delete everything in the app in one tap, or by e-mail.

Bosnify is a free language-learning app for learning Bosnian, made by an individual developer with no commercial aim. It has no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no purchases. This policy explains what the app stores, where, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Your account

The first time Bosnify goes online it creates an anonymous account for you. It does not ask for an e-mail address, phone number or password. The account has a random ID and a six-character friend code.

Optional: Google sign-in. In Profile you can link a Google account, so your progress survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone. If you do, Google tells Bosnify your e-mail address and your name and profile picture as set in Google. Bosnify uses the e-mail address only to sign you in and to show you which account is linked; your Google name and picture are kept by the sign-in service but not shown or used. Bosnify never sees your Google password.

The server is run by Supabase in the European Union (Frankfurt). Data travels encrypted (HTTPS).

Who can see what

  • Your friends can see your display name, avatar, total and this week's XP, your day streak, the lesson you are on, and your XP from the last three days in their activity feed.
  • Every Bosnify user can see the weekly league: display name, avatar and this week's XP of the top learners.
  • Your e-mail address and your progress backup are visible only to you.
  • No one else sees your data. Bosnify does not sell or share data, and has no advertising or analytics partners.

What stays on your device

Lesson progress, daily quests, stories read, your interface language and your streak reminders are stored on your device. Reminders are scheduled on the phone itself; Bosnify does not use push notifications, so no device token leaves your phone. Pronunciations are recordings built into the app; a word without a recording is read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech. Bosnify itself sends nothing you do to a voice or AI service.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Profile → Delete my account and progress. This deletes your account and everything the server holds about you at once (profile, XP, friendships, gifts, progress backup and the Google link), and resets the app.
  • Without the app: e-mail hasankaankarabulut121@gmail.com from any address and tell us your display name or friend code (Profile shows it), or the Google e-mail you linked. We delete everything within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.
  • Only on this phone: Profile → Sign out keeps the account and its backup on the server, so you can sign in again later.

Feedback

If you send feedback from the app (Profile) or the website, your message, and your e-mail address if you choose to add one, is passed to Web3Forms (web3forms.com), which e-mails it to us. It is not stored in Bosnify's database or linked to your account. We use it only to read and answer your feedback, and delete the e-mail when it is no longer needed.

Children

Bosnify is meant for learners aged 13 and over. It does not ask for personal information; a display name is optional, and there is no chat or free text other than that name.

Changes
